My Story

I started exploring Python and programming out of curiosity — and quickly discovered a genuine passion for it. That moment changed everything. I made the decision to pivot, and I have been fully committed to learning backend development ever since.

I am currently building a solid foundation in Python, Django, and software engineering principles, with a focus on writing clean, maintainable code.
